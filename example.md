# Fictional example: "The Quiet Mind Quiz"

*Fictional coach, fictional offers. For orientation only.*

**Lead magnet:** The Quiet Mind Quiz (instant, 5 questions, 4 minutes)
**Length:** about 340 words · 2:20
**Buttons:** 1. Breathing Room membership, $27 per month · 2. Free clarity conversation (booking page)

---

## 1 · Confirmation + joy (0:00 to 0:10)
> You did it. Your Quiet Mind Quiz is waiting for you, and I'm really glad you're here. I'm Lena. Watch this short video to the end, it matters.

## 2 · The ONE next step (0:10 to 0:30)
> Take the quiz today. Not someday. Five questions, four minutes, just for you. Trust your momentum: there is a reason you clicked right now.

## 3 · Mirroring (0:30 to 0:55)
> If you're here, I already know a few things about you. You run a busy practice and your clients love you. From the outside, everything works. And inside, late at night, you think: "Everyone gets my calm. Nobody gets my calm for me." You wish you could breathe again in your own life, not only in your sessions.

## 4 · Naming (0:55 to 1:05)
> Nothing is wrong with you. You have been holding space for others for so long that your own space got very small. And you can take it back, one breath at a time.

## 5 · Invitation (1:05 to 1:10)
> If you recognise yourself in this, you'll find two buttons below this video. Let me tell you briefly what's behind them.

## 6 · Button 1: the small step (1:10 to 1:40)
> The first button is my Breathing Room membership. Inside you'll find a ten-minute evening practice, a weekly live circle and a quiet community of women who get it. It's 27 dollars a month. If you can't join the live circle, the recording is there for you. If you want to go deeper right away instead of waiting, this is your place.

## 7 · Button 2: the conversation (1:40 to 2:05)
> The second button is for you if you feel: I don't want to figure this out alone anymore, and I don't want to wait until after the quiz to decide. Behind it, you choose a date for a free clarity conversation with me. We look at where you are, where you want to go, and whether I can walk with you. Whether we work together afterwards or not, you leave with clarity.

## 8 · Recap + release (2:05 to 2:20)
> So: take your quiz today. If you want to go deeper right away, join Breathing Room. If you want to walk this way together with me, book your conversation. Either way, I'm so glad you're here.
